# Memo: Bank Stress Watchlist, 2026 Q2

**To:** Risk Committee
**From:** Dhairya Shah
**Date:** October 23, 2026
**Re:** SVB-style risk screen of US banks over $1B in assets

## Bottom line

The screen ranks 987 US banks over $1B on six stress metrics drawn from public Call Reports. Tested on data available at the end of 2022, it placed all four bank failures over $1B since 2023 in its top 12%, and two of them in its top 5. On the 2026 Q2 data, the main risk on the watchlist is funding and liquidity, not solvency. Two banks fall below well-capitalized levels under a rate shock of just +100bp.

## Top risks

**1. A modest rate rise pushes two banks below the line.** If bond losses are fully recognized:
- Independence Bank of Kentucky (#9, $3.8B): adjusted Tier 1 is already 3.9% of total assets, below the 5% threshold. It falls to 1.9% at +100bp and about 0% at +200bp. Its unrealized bond losses already equal 57% of Tier 1 capital.
- American National Bank of Texas (#10, $5.5B): adjusted Tier 1 falls from 7.4% to 5.5% of risk-weighted assets at +100bp, below the 6% threshold, and to 3.5% at +200bp.

The other eight top 10 banks stay above 6% even at +200bp.

**2. Thin liquidity against uninsured deposits.** This is the pattern that sank SVB and Signature.
- North American Banking Company (#4, $1.5B): 81% of deposits are uninsured.
- Reading Co-operative Bank (#5, $1.2B): cash plus unpledged securities cover only 4% of uninsured deposits.
- First Hawaiian Bank (#8, $23.6B): liquid assets cover only 20% of uninsured deposits, with 48% of deposits uninsured.

**3. Commercial real estate concentration.** Four of the top 10 hold CRE above the 300%-of-capital supervisory threshold: Simmons Bank (345%), Union Bank (335%), Inwood National Bank (325%) and Texas Regional Bank (312%).

## Banks to prioritize

| Priority | Bank | Assets | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Independence Bank of Kentucky | $3.8B | Already below 5% on an adjusted basis |
| 2 | American National Bank of Texas | $5.5B | Breaks at +100bp |
| 3 | Simmons Bank | $24.7B | Largest name on the list, CRE at 345%, liquidity coverage of 25% |
| 4 | First Hawaiian Bank | $23.6B | Large uninsured base with low liquidity coverage |
| 5 | Reading Co-operative Bank | $1.2B | Lowest liquidity coverage on the list |

## What to watch next quarter

- **Deposit outflows.** Two straight quarters of falling deposits at any priority bank should trigger a closer review.
- **Rate path.** A +100bp move is enough to push American National Bank of Texas below 6%.
- **CRE refinancing.** Office and multifamily loans repricing in 2026 and 2027 at the four banks above 300%.
- **2026 Q3 Call Reports.** Rerun the screen as soon as they are filed.

## Limitations

The backtest rests on only four failures, so treat it as a sanity check, not proof. Mortgage bond durations are assumptions. The data is at the bank level, not the holding company level. Reading Co-operative's 4% liquidity figure is unusually low and should be checked against the original filing before any action.
